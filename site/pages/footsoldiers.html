HEAT FOOTSOLDIERS

Dedicated HEAT members spread the word, registered new users and took part in Footsoldier-only contests and raids.

COMMUNITY PROGRAM

WHAT THE SOURCE SAYS

  • Footsoldiers devoted time and energy to recruiting others to HEAT.
  • Each new user registered by a Footsoldier earned that member 500 Degrees.
  • Footsoldier-only raids and contests offered prizes including trips to E3, HEAT headquarters and a paintball outing in Pennsylvania.
  • The program was open to Premium and Perimeter members through a recruiting-office form.
