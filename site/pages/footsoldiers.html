FOOTSOLDIERS

Welcome to Footsoldiers, HEAT.NET's exclusive strategy mini-game! Build your squad, complete missions, and climb the ranks to become a legendary commander in the HEAT community.

GAME FEATURES

⚔️ TACTICAL MISSIONS
Deploy your squad on strategic missions to earn points, degrees, and exclusive rewards.
🎖️ MILITARY RANKS
Rise through the ranks from Private to General as you complete missions and prove your strategic prowess.
💰 HEAT DEGREES
Earn HEAT Degrees (the network currency) to spend in the HEAT Store or use for premium features.
🏆 PLAYER OF THE MONTH
Top-ranking Footsoldiers commanders compete monthly for exclusive trophies and recognition.
🎯 AMMUNITION & HQ MANAGEMENT
Manage your resources, upgrade your HQ, and stock ammunition for more challenging missions.
⚡ REAL-TIME VICTORIES
Track your wins in real-time and see your name on the Footsoldiers victory boards.

HISTORICAL NOTE

This is a restored historical page from HEAT.NET (1997-2003). Footsoldiers was a popular mini-game that allowed HEAT members to engage in turn-based strategy gameplay while earning HEAT Degrees (the network currency). The original game interface used framesets and server-side logic that cannot be fully replicated in this restoration, but the game concept and design are preserved here for historical reference.
