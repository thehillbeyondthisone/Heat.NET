WHAT ARE HEAT DEGREES?

HEAT Degrees are the official currency of the HEAT.NET gaming network. Launched in 1997, Degrees revolutionized online gaming by creating a unified economy across all games and services.

Key Features:
• Earn Degrees through gameplay, tournaments, and achievements
• Spend Degrees on games, tournaments, memberships, and more
• Trade Degrees with other HEAT members
• Purchase Degrees packs in the HEAT Store
• Track your balance and transactions in real-time
• Integrated with all HEAT games and services

Current Exchange Rate: Approximately $1 USD = 1,000 HEAT Degrees
(Rates may vary based on purchase amount and promotions)

Historical Note: HEAT Degrees was one of the first virtual currencies in online gaming (1997-2003). It pioneered the concept of a unified gaming economy where players could earn in-game currency through gameplay and spend it across the entire network. This restoration shows simulated account data and transaction history. The original system supported real-time balance updates, peer-to-peer trading, and integration with payment processors for purchasing Degrees.
