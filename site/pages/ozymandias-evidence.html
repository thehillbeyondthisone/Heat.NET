The 1999 Ozymandias promotion

PAGE OZYMANDIAS

ARCHIVED RULE October 5, 1999 · Ways to Win Degrees

The preserved HEAT page says OZYMANDIAS logged on once each day. The first member to page him after he logged on received 10,000 DEGREES.

“If you are the first person to PAGE him after he’s logged on, you get 10,000 DEGREES.”

Original Wayback capture · Preserved source HTML

THE LOCAL RECREATION

The host PC can open one five-minute visit per America/Chicago calendar day. OZYMANDIAS appears as a labeled event contact, and the first signed-in account to page him receives 10,000 Degrees in its existing statement. A durable winner record prevents repeated awards after retries or restarts.

The original visit time, duration, timezone, client appearance and network protocol are not established by this source. The host control, five-minute window, Chicago day, buddy list, offline delivery and browser UI are reconstruction choices. The prize amount and first-page rule come from the archive.
