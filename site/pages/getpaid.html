ORIGINALHistorical earning rates

GET PAID TO PLAY

HEAT awarded frequent-player points called Degrees. The original page described earning them through games, lobbies, events and community programs, then redeeming them through the Store.

DETAILED EARNING SCHEDULE

Rates stated on the archived GET PAID TO PLAY page.
Historical ruleDegrees
Log in and launch a game, once per day100
Every 60 minutes playing a game250
Launch a game on 15 days in a month5,000
Launch a game every day in a month10,000
Launch a game every day in a year500,000

The introductory copy also mentions time in game lobbies. The detailed schedule says game play. This public page preserves the detailed schedule; it does not record gameplay.

OTHER HISTORICAL SOURCES OF DEGREES

  • Thank Goodness It’s Fragdays

    Weekend action events advertised 100,000 Degrees “up for grabs.” This is an event prize description, not an automatic account grant.

  • Trivia Trio

    Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday trivia windows advertised 45,000 Degrees in available prizes.

  • HEAT Footsoldiers

    The article says a Footsoldier received 500 Degrees for each new user they registered. It does not describe tactical-mission payouts.

  • Monthly Links Program

    HEAT advertised 10,000 Degrees for selected user-created links to the network.

PRESERVED REWARDS GUIDE

The rates and event descriptions above come from the captured HEAT page. This public restoration does not award Degrees, redeem points or provide a cash conversion.
