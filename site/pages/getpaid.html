How do you earn Degrees?
You accumulate Degrees by:
  • Playing your favorite games or chatting in game lobbies
  • Get 250 Degrees for every hour you are in a game or game lobby

HEAT allows Premium members to CASH IN Degrees for PC and Console GAMES, SOFTWARE, HARDWARE, GAME GUIDES, and more at the HEAT Store. How do you know how many Degrees you have?

When you register on HEAT for the first time, a Degree account is created under your username, and can track the Degrees you accumulate. Check the middle of your Homebase page for the number of Degrees you have accumulated. Or, check out the HEAT Store and see how much your Degrees are worth in cold, hard cash.
