What Is 10SIX?

The next step in online gaming, 10SIX is specifically designed for massive team play over the Internet, where you are against up to 999,999 of your closest friends and enemies in the biggest land rush in history.

10SIX operates 24 hours, 7 days a week, even when you're not signed on! It's a world where weapons and equipment can be bought, sold and stolen, where four corporations struggle for planetary dominance, and you struggle to survive.
