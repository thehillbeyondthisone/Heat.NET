FIDELITY RULES

  • Charter Member requires actual membership at the network’s 1997 launch; it is not a generic account badge.
  • 10SIX Tester of the Week and Premium Player of the Month were selected community awards, not automatic gameplay achievements.
  • The Holiday trophy belongs to its historical login events. No surviving artwork is substituted.
  • 21102.gif is an advertisement, not a Premium Member trophy, and is excluded from the catalog.
  • The downloaded 6101.gif and 6144.gif files are HTML error pages. Their descriptions survive; their art does not.
Homebase distinction: the public site displays historical criteria and artwork; it does not award trophies or publish private member records.
