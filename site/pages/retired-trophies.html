Retired HEAT Trophies

These trophies have been retired and are no longer available to earn. They represent historic milestones and achievements from HEAT's golden era. If you see a player with one of these trophies, they've been with HEAT since the early days.

Charter Member Trophy
If you visit a Homebase that contains the Charter Member Trophy, it means the user who holds it was with HEAT at the very beginning, when the network first went LIVE in 1997. They are the first pioneers to play on HEAT, helping to make it what it is today. The Charter Member trophy was retired on Dec. 31, 1997.
Retired: December 31, 1997


Feel the HEAT Trophy
When we first launched our deal with @Home, we hosted an @Home Event to kick off our new partnership. Users who participated in the event earned this stylish "@Home Feels the Heat" Trophy. The Feel the HEAT Trophy was retired in April of 1998.
Retired: April 1998


"The Movie" Trophy
We just couldn't let the opening of Episode 1: The Phantom Menace go by without marking it in the HEAT history books. So, in celebration of what was one of the most anticipated movies of all time, we awarded every user who either played a Star Wars game on HEAT the day Phantom Menace released in the theatres OR sent us their Phantom Menace ticket stub via snail mail by June 19th, this commemorative Star Wars Homebase Trophy. "The Movie" Trophy was retired in July of 1999.
Retired: July 1999


10SIX Tester of the Week
The 10SIX Tester of the Week Trophy used to go to one user a week for testing Heat's awesome game 10SIX. They are users who exhibited excellent participation, willingly help others, and display an overall commitment to making the game ROCK. The 10SIX Tester Trophy was retired in March of 2000.
Retired: March 2000


Hellcamp Ref Trophy
HELLCAMP Ref's are HEAT PREMIUM Members who applied and were picked to run Raids, Trivia Events and more in an installment of the HEAT HellCamp Program. If you see someone bearing this Trophy, he/she was picked as a Ref in the past Hellcamp Program. The last Hellcamp program was held in 1999. The Hellcamp Ref Trophy was retired in March of 2000.
Retired: March 2000


Premium Player of the Month
The POTM Trophy was awarded to PREMIUM Members whose contributions to the network have been so great, that the PREMIUM Player Of The Week Trophy just isn't enough to honor them. Not only did the PREMIUM Player Of The Month get this cool trophy, but they also got a large Degree prize from our friends at the Barons!
Retired: 2000
