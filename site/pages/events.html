EVENT DETAILS:

T.G.I.F. - Thank Goodness It's Fragdays
 Every Friday night, late night gamers unite for the biggest FPS party of the week! HEAT Prize Personalities host massive multiplayer sessions across Quake II, Unreal Tournament, Half-Life, and more. 100,000 Degrees prize pool distributed to top fraggers, most improved players, and random participants. No entry fee - just show up and frag!

 Trivia Trio
 Three times per week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday), HEAT's trivia personalities visit game lobbies to test your gaming knowledge. Questions range from game lore to industry history to pop culture. 45,000 Degrees total weekly prizes! Look for personalities: HEATriviaLord, HEATriviaGoddess, HEATriviaSpaz, HEATriviaPunk, HEATriviaDiva, and HEATriviaMonger.

 10SIX Corporation Wars
 Every Sunday, corporations battle for territorial supremacy on Planet Visitor! Coordinate with your corp members for massive PvP battles. Winning corporation members each receive Degrees bonuses and exclusive trophies. Base defense, tactical strikes, and all-out warfare - this is 10SIX at its finest!

 How to Participate:
