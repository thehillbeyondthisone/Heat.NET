ABOUT THIS RESTORATION

This is a local, source-preserving reconstruction of HEAT.NET’s 10SIX era—not a reopened commercial network and not a claim that every archived service has returned.

THREE LABELS GOVERN THE SITE

ORIGINALSurviving wording, artwork, rules or page structure supported by the preserved HEAT captures.
RESTOREDA working modern implementation of a historical idea: the local game launcher, Pioneer profile, reports, comparisons and Degree ledger.
EXPANSIONNew Visitor features such as First Claim and Two-Camp Pioneer. They are deliberately not presented as recovered HEAT awards.

WHAT WORKS LOCALLY

  • The 10SIX channel reads validated reports from the local native game without advancing or rewriting the save.
  • Homebase shows the selected Pioneer, owned camps, HEAT Degrees, real recorded activity and earned Visitor-expansion trophies.
  • Players can search, sort and compare local or manually imported report snapshots. An imported snapshot does not create online presence.
  • PLAY 10SIX NOW opens the prepared local game. The browser is a front door, not the game runtime.

CURRENT BOUNDARIES

  • No public multiplayer server, online HEAT accounts, live chat, Pager messaging, trading, store checkout or cash redemption is present.
  • The historical game-directory counts are preserved as archive facts; most listed games are not supplied or hosted.
  • Historical trophies retain their original event, membership and community-selection requirements. Camp statistics cannot retroactively earn them.
  • Some downloaded “image” files are archive error pages. The working site omits them instead of disguising replacements as originals.
  • The untouched archive may still contain broken Wayback URLs and missing remote assets. Those failures are part of the surviving record.
